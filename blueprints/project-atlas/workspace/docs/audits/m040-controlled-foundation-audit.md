# M040 Controlled Foundation Audit

## Controls

- M040 is the only new Partner Registry; consuming modules keep Partner IDs instead of duplicating partner records.
- The Partner Gate requires active verified partner, capability, jurisdiction, authorization and agreement before material work.
- Onboarding cannot complete with unmet required evidence.
- Assignments are idempotent and blocked while external operations are disabled.
- Suspension blocks new work without rewriting or destroying active journeys.
- Economics require active agreement and qualifying evidence; they do not affect client payment, organic ranking or quality.
- AI cannot make lifecycle, authorization, verification or economic decisions.
