# M017 CRM — UX/UI and experience specification

- Owner: Codex Architecture Agent using UI/UX Pro Max
- Final approver: Product Owner
- Status: Product/Architecture design candidate; no visual Build gate
- Surface: Admin / Internal Platform
- Brand baseline: Manrope, Inter, navy/cobalt/cyan/green/gold, light-first, subtle motion
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA and reduced-motion support

## 1. Experience objective

M017 must let an authorized operator answer five questions quickly:

1. Who needs attention?
2. What commercial relationship or opportunity is this?
3. Who owns the next action and when is it due?
4. Which facts are canonical, current and authorized?
5. What safe action may I take next?

The experience sentence is:

> Understand the relationship, advance the opportunity and hand work to the owning module without
> losing context or inventing authority.

Although SG Solutions will initially be operated by the Product Owner alone, the design must not
hard-code owner-wide visibility or a one-user information model. It should feel focused for one
operator and remain compatible with later team-scoped roles.

## 2. Brand translation

The SG Solutions logo combines financial growth, business and home ownership. The CRM translates
that identity into calm professional momentum rather than a flashy sales dashboard:

- Manrope for page titles, opportunity names, stage headings and primary values.
- Inter for tables, labels, notes, metadata, filters and system messages.
- Navy `#0A2540` for primary hierarchy and text.
- Cobalt `#0B63CE` for focused navigation and primary actions.
- Cyan `#00A3E0` for restrained informational accents, never ordinary body text on white.
- Green `#2E7D32` only for verified positive outcomes with text/icon reinforcement.
- Gold `#B7791F` only as a controlled brand accent; it is not the sole warning color.
- Surface `#F7F9FC` for quiet grouping over white working panels.

Cards use 12–16px radii, low-contrast borders and shallow elevation. Metallic gradients, oversized
financial imagery, decorative charts, celebration confetti, alarm animation and gamified pressure
are prohibited. The supplied logo appears once in the authenticated shell with approved clear space
and is not stretched, cropped, tiled or used as a watermark.

The exact route and labels remain `CRM-002`:

│   ├── My work / overview
│   ├── Leads (M020 projection)
│   ├── Contacts / relationships
│   ├── Clients (M018 projection)
│   ├── Organizations (M019 projection)
│   ├── Opportunities
│   ├── Pipeline
│   ├── Activities

M017 does not add top-level items for every entity. The CRM relationship detail composes authorized
references, while canonical detail remains in the owning module.

## 4. Primary screens

### 4.1 CRM overview / My work

- Page title and concise localized explanation.
- Scope chip showing `My work`, approved team or approved organization scope.
- Adjacent list-scope control showing the approved stable logical purpose/service ID and localized
  label at the registry/`asOf`. It never selects
  one relationship-owned binding across rows: the server resolves and returns the exact authorized
  active binding ref/version/access epoch independently for every row. There is no implicit primary/
  default and no cross-purpose aggregation.
- Search/filter entry with no sensitive value persisted in the URL.
- Summary of unassigned items, overdue next actions, upcoming actions and stale/blocked work.
- Priority list with explanation codes, owner/queue, stage, service interest and due time.
- No decorative revenue totals or inferred zero when M020/M092/payment data is unavailable.
- A separately permissioned `Proposed service relationships` queue shows only minimized purpose/
  service, received time and immutable M020/M018 owner-evidence receipt status. It is the sole special
  read path for a proposed binding and never reveals ordinary relationship detail, counts or contact
  values.
- Proposal detail rereads typed owner evidence, shows binding/policy versions and offers explicit
  `Activate`, `Reject` or `Leave pending` according to capability. Activate/Reject require reason/
  evidence, show blocked/stale/owner-unavailable states, final-fence current versions and never create
  consent/contactability. After completion, focus moves to the durable result notice; only an active
  binding can enter normal CRM work.

### 4.2 Relationships list

- Table on desktop; prioritized cards on narrow screens.
- Default visible fields: safe display label, the selected row binding's localized purpose/service
  plus commercial engagement lifecycle, owner/queue, opportunity summary, next action, last
  permitted activity and freshness. The row carries the exact binding ref/version/access epoch;
  this display-only projection never creates a root-level relationship type or infers Client state.
- Contact values are masked unless the actor has field-specific purpose and permission.
- Every list query/cursor is visibly scoped to one stable logical purpose/service ID and binds the
  server's binding-registry version/`asOf`; each row carries its own exact binding ref/version/access
  epoch and immutable applied definition code/version. Publishing/upgrading a definition does not
  change scope, hide or grant a row, or silently remap historical meaning; registry change
  invalidates the cursor and the server re-resolves each row.
  Switching purpose clears selection, counts, filters, cursors and cached rows before the newly
  authorized query runs. Exact binding selection begins only after one relationship/detail/action is
  chosen.
- Column chooser/saved views appear only after governance approval. A saved view displays its exact
  dataset, stable purpose/service and owner/team scope. Schema/filter/purpose/team-receipt drift makes
  it unavailable with a repair route; the UI never drops a filter or broadens to all CRM.
- General bulk CRM-record selection/mutation is absent in 1A/1B and Future until a later concrete
  command receives its own Product Owner-approved PRD/gate. Export and separately owned campaign
  delivery do not activate bulk record mutation.

### 4.3 Opportunity pipeline

- Board and equivalent list view share one canonical query and transition contract.
- Stage columns show authorized item counts only; suppressed/partial/unavailable is explicit.
- Cards minimize visible PII and show relationship label, service interest, owner, next action,
  age/staleness and structured blockers.
- An organization label is rendered only from the Opportunity's exact current M019 relationship
  receipt. End/revoke shows a generic stale-context blocker and requires explicit authorized
  correction; no preferred organization is substituted.
- Drag-and-drop is never the only transition. Keyboard/menu transition opens the same preview.
- Before commit, the transition dialog shows source stage/version, destination, required missing
  fields, reason, downstream non-effects and confirmation.
- `won` copy explicitly says it does not confirm payment or authorize service execution.

### 4.4 Relationship 360

Header:

- safe display identity and relationship state;
- exact selected purpose/service binding, status and freshness; changing it reloads every section;
- owner/queue and next action;
- source/freshness/partial-state indicators;
- allowlisted actions based on current authorization.

Tabs/sections use only the closed versioned section registry:

- Overview: relationship and opportunity summary.
- Opportunities: M017 canonical detail.
- Activity: CRM-owned items plus typed owner references.
- Notes: separately authorized internal notes.
- Leads: M020 projection.
- Client / household: M018 projection and reauthorized link.
- Organization: M019 projection and reauthorized link.
- Services/cases/tasks: M021–M023 projections.
- Appointments/messages/payments/documents: minimized owner summaries and links.
- Audit: bounded M077 projection for authorized reviewers, not raw infrastructure logs.

Each section calls one typed owner port with exact refs/versions/purpose/classification/grants/access
epochs and bounded freshness. It can be complete, partial, stale, unavailable, suppressed, denied,
unknown or not applicable independently. Requested-but-unauthorized sections disclose no count or
existence; one incomplete requested section keeps the aggregate partial. No free-form section may
trigger an unspecified fan-out. Drill-down uses an opaque route and reauthorizes. The Leads tab is
explicitly an M020 qualification projection; no CRM stage/tag/score is shown as qualification.
Sections never aggregate facts across purpose bindings.
Any `new/engaged/active client/at risk/former client` indicator is explicitly labeled as an
explained commercial/operational projection under `CRM-008`; it is not credit risk, and partial
inputs never produce a definitive label.

### 4.5 Opportunity detail

- Current versioned pipeline/stage with history.
- Requested service and relationship reference.
- Qualification checklist and unknown values without forced guesses.
- Owner/queue, priority reason and next action.
- A linked Task shows only the M023-authorized minimized status/due/owner projection and its opaque
  route. Stale/corrected/deleted/reassigned/revoked links disappear or show a generic stale-link
  notice after server confirmation; M017 never completes the Task from this control.
- Authorized assignment history uses a paginated minimized panel with old/new owner or queue,
  structured reason and effective time; viewing it never grants assignment authority.
- Structured close/reopen action.
- Conversion preview shows the approved dependency graph/order (for example Client → ServiceOrder →
  CaseFile only when CRM-004/005 approves it), exact owner modules, and which prerequisite is
  satisfied, reused, unknown, blocked or ambiguous. Dependents are visibly `dependency blocked` and
  are not shown as attempted.
- Outcome receipt distinguishes not started, ready, dependency blocked, accepted, no-op reuse,
  blocked, conflict, unavailable, ambiguous and reconciled without implying parallel execution.
- Authorized staff can reopen a durable conversion status showing minimized per-owner outcomes. An
  explicit `Reconcile` action checks ambiguous steps; `Resume` is offered only for proven not-started
  work after a fresh binding/owner fence. It never tells staff to rerun conversion as a new request.
  Resume cannot bypass dependency predicates and its confirmation names only proven-not-started steps.

### 4.6 Data quality and duplicates

- The queue separates duplicate candidates from missing-required, invalid-format, canonical-
  conflict, stale-source and invalid-source issues.
- Each issue shows masked reason/evidence, canonical owner, source version/freshness, responsible
  reviewer, status and safe owner-module route. M017 may assign/defer/recheck/resolve from an owner
  receipt but cannot edit the protected canonical fact inline.
- Candidate queue exposes reason codes and masked comparisons only.
- `Distinct`, `Defer` and `Request canonical resolution` are separate actions.
- Merge preview is a high-risk screen: affected graph, authoritative winner, conflicts, aliases,
  sessions/access implications, recovery reference and unavailable owners.
- No score-only action, no name-only merge and no destructive “delete duplicate” shortcut.
- Confirmation does not use dark patterns and cannot be triggered through drag/swipe.
- Opportunity candidates are labeled separately and never use the Person/Client merge screen. Their
  enhanced preview offers only approved `Keep both`, `Link as related` or `Supersede commercial
  record`; it shows exact Opportunity versions and the stage/assignment/activity/attribution/next-
  action histories that remain. The receipt exposes durable outcome/recovery without implying a
  Person, Client, account, session or grant change.
- Canonical merge and Opportunity-resolution operations have separately authorized durable status
  views with minimized step outcomes. `Reconcile` performs owner lookup; enhanced `Resume` appears
  only for a current approved recovery plan and proven not-started steps. The UI never recommends
  rerunning Execute, and frozen/manual-recovery state remains unmistakable.

### 4.7 Internal notes

- Note type, purpose and visibility are selected from approved values.
- Plain text by default; rich text only with an approved sanitizer profile.
- Persistent reminder: internal, never client-visible, and not a substitute for a task/message.
- Saving, saved, superseded, denied and encryption/service-unavailable states are distinct.
- No automatic translation, analytics capture or AI use.

### 4.8 Imports and exports (Release 1B)

Import is a stepper: file requirements → quarantine/scan → mapping → validation/deduplication
preview → explicit apply → durable row/owner receipt. A rejected file never advances. Partial or
ambiguous outcomes open a reconciliation view before retry. Only an enhanced-authorized, versioned
compensation-plan preview can offer supported reversals; the result uses `Compensation pending`,
`Compensated`, `Partially compensated` or `Manual recovery required`, identifies irreversible effects
without exposing protected content, and never says full rollback.

Export is a guarded dialog: approved dataset → fields → reason/purpose → current scope summary →
step-up if required → generate → short-lived private download. It warns that authorization is
rechecked at generation and download.

## 5. Navigation and responsive behavior

### Desktop, 1280px and wider

- Global Admin rail; CRM secondary navigation appears as tabs or a compact subrail.
- List/board/detail may use split view only when it does not expose hidden records during selection.
- Dense tables preserve 200% zoom and reflow before horizontal scroll becomes the primary mode.

### Tablet, 768–1279px

- Collapsible global navigation.
- Pipeline defaults to list-with-stage grouping; board remains optional.
- Filters open in a dismissible sheet and preserve an accessible summary.

### Mobile, below 768px

- One-column prioritized cards; no miniature desktop board.
- Sticky bottom action only for the single most relevant permitted action and never over content.
- Contact method reveal/copy is explicit; values are not placed in page titles or persistent shell.
- Complex transitions, merge, import and export use full-screen controlled flows.

Breakpoints are implementation tokens, not assumptions about a specific device. All critical
journeys must work with keyboard, touch, screen reader and 200% zoom.

On overview/list/pipeline surfaces, `PurposeBindingSelector` renders as a stable logical purpose/
service scope
selector and never exposes one binding ID as a cross-row filter. On a selected relationship it
exposes only exact bindings the server authorizes. Proposed bindings cannot authorize ordinary CRM
reads/actions; only the separately permissioned, minimized proposal-review query and Activate/Reject
commands may reference them.
Expired, revoked or superseded detail selection immediately clears protected UI and offers a generic
refresh/reselection path; a stale access epoch never falls back to another binding. The applicable
scope/binding label and status are programmatically associated with lists, sections, dialogs and
commands.

## 7. State language

Use distinct, localized states:

| State | Meaning | UX behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Complete | Owner-confirmed authorized result | Show value and source time |
| Partial | Some authorized owners failed/omitted | Show coverage and safe drill-down |
| Stale | Result exceeds source freshness policy | Warn and offer authorized refresh |
| Unavailable | Trustworthy result cannot be produced | Never substitute zero/none |
| Suppressed | Policy withholds count/value | Explain generally without threshold leakage |
| Denied | Actor lacks permission/purpose | No existence or count disclosure |
| Unknown | Business fact is not established | Ask/review; never guess |
| Not applicable | Approved policy says the fact does not apply | Explain neutrally; never map to complete, zero or denied |

Purpose-binding state uses explicit localized `Proposed`, `Active`, `Expired`, `Revoked` and
`Superseded` text rather than color alone. Revocation/stale-epoch recovery moves focus to a concise
status notice and returns to the authorized selector; hidden alternatives are not announced or
counted.

`Client`, `won`, `paid`, `authorized to start`, `in progress` and `complete` use separate labels and
icons. No optimistic UI may visually advance a high-risk state before the server receipt.

## 8. Interaction and motion

- 120–180ms opacity/position transitions for low-risk UI only.
- Pipeline movement waits for confirmed server response; pending state remains in the origin with a
  clear busy indicator.
- Reduced-motion removes movement while preserving focus and state feedback.
- Skeletons preserve layout and contain no fabricated values.
- Toasts supplement, never replace, inline errors and durable receipts.
- Destructive/high-risk actions require explicit labels and consequence summaries; never color-only
  or vague `OK` buttons.

## 9. Forms and validation

- Progressive forms ask only fields required for the current approved service/stage.
- Required/optional/prohibited classification comes from a versioned registry, not JSX.
- Validation appears beside the field and in an accessible summary; focus moves predictably.
- User-entered text remains verbatim; normalized/search/match representations are server-derived.
- Autosave is limited to approved low-risk drafts. Transition, conversion, merge, import apply and
  export are explicit commands with version/idempotency protection.
- Phone/email validation never asserts identity, consent or deliverability.

## 10. Search, filters and URLs

- Search is server-authorized before matching, counts and pagination.
- Sensitive queries are not stored in URL parameters, browser history, analytics or local storage.
- Saved views persist allowlisted filter definitions, never result rows or contact values.
- Filter pills state the active authorized scope; clearing filters cannot broaden permission.
- No-result copy distinguishes “no authorized matches” from system failure without revealing hidden
  records.

## 11. Accessibility verification matrix

| Journey | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Navigate CRM | landmarks, skip link, current-page announcement, mobile menu focus trap/return |
| Browse relationships | table/card semantics, screen-reader labels, 200% zoom, focus persistence |
| Move stage | keyboard/menu parity, required-field summary, live success/error, no color-only state |
| Assign/next action | accessible combobox/date-time, IANA-zone label, conflict recovery |
| Review activity | ordered semantics, source/freshness text, links with descriptive names |
| Resolve duplicate | masked comparison, conflict headings, explicit confirmation and error recovery |
| Import/export | step progress, scan/validation status, row-error summary, secure-download semantics |
| Review binding proposal | keyboard Activate/Reject/Leave pending, immutable evidence state, consequence text, durable status, focus restored after conflict/expiry |
| Reveal protected contact | explicit purpose/reason, no unexpected screen-reader announcement of PII, expiry/revocation clears value and restores focus safely |
| Close/reopen Opportunity | dependency/relation/handoff consequences, keyboard confirmation, live durable status and stale-sibling recovery |
| Convert/reconcile/resume | ordered DAG semantics, prerequisite and dependency-blocked text, no false parallel/success state, proven-step confirmation and recovery focus |
| Create/supersede/redact note | revision/retention/legal-hold consequences, editor/error labels, destructive confirmation and safe focus without reading body unexpectedly |
| Binding access ended | frozen-state explanation, no ordinary controls, privileged remediation plan/outcomes and recoverable focus after blocked/conflict |

Every row is verified in EN/ES with keyboard, supported screen reader, 200% zoom and reduced motion;
timeout/revocation/conflict restores focus to a safe durable-status heading or initiating control.
Automated checks supplement manual keyboard, screen reader, zoom, contrast and reduced-motion tests.

## 12. Bilingual content rules

- English and Spanish ship together for all system-controlled CRM UI.
- Labels avoid unexplained sales/legal/financial jargon; help text explains `lead`, `opportunity`,
  `client` and `case` distinctions.
- Locale-neutral codes drive workflows; translation changes do not change business state.
- Staff notes and imported free text show original language and are not auto-translated.
- Date/time presentation includes locale and IANA zone. Numeric/currency display never invents an
  opportunity value or financial authority.
- Material confirmation/disclosure copy is versioned and Product Owner approved.

## 13. Privacy-safe display

- Default lists use safe display labels and masked contact methods.
- Full contact values require a protected-field reveal; future policy may make it timed, audited,
  no-store, visibly watermarked/contextual and excluded from ordinary export/automatic copy. A web
  application cannot reliably prevent operating-system or camera screenshots, so the design instead
  minimizes fields and reveal duration and relies on policy/training/audit without promising capture
  prevention.
- Reveal is an explicit button/menu action for one labeled field, never hover/focus/row selection.
  The server response is no-store and the value is removed on navigation, blur timeout, binding/
  assurance change or revocation. Automatic clipboard copy is absent; copy, watermark and TTL remain
  gated policy. Screen-reader status announces reveal/expiry without reading the value unexpectedly.
  The transient value and opaque M077 audit receipt are never conflated; the receipt cannot reveal or
  replay the value. Allowed, denied and failed attempts are reviewed only through an authorized M077
  projection, not an M017 history panel.
- No PII in breadcrumbs, tabs, document titles, browser notifications, error text, telemetry or
  copied debug IDs.
- Timeline summaries are type- and source-based (`Appointment scheduled`) rather than body previews.
- Hidden fields are never shipped to the browser.

## 14. Empty, loading and failure states

- New system/no CRM records: setup guidance without fake examples presented as real.
- Filtered empty: active filter summary and safe clear action.
- Denied: generic permission guidance; no hidden count.
- Partial/unavailable owner: preserve usable sections, label source/freshness and offer retry/manual
  route.
- Stale command: retain user input, explain record changed and require review before resubmit.
- Conversion/merge/import ambiguity: durable receipt and manual recovery route; no infinite spinner.

## 15. Measurable targets pending approval

`CRM-021` must approve target devices, dataset sizes, response budgets, accessibility evidence and
error-recovery expectations before Build. Documentary defaults:

- critical daily work is reachable in no more than three meaningful navigation actions;
- no critical journey depends on drag, hover, color, animation or desktop width;
- loading does not reveal unauthorized counts or fabricated zero;
- sensitive mutation feedback is durable and recoverable;
- board/list/detail preserve context without leaking it into URLs or telemetry.
