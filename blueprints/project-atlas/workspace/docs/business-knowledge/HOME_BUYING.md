# Home Buying Knowledge

SG Solutions prepares and educates prospective buyers about credit, income, employment, debt,
budget, reserves, down payment, closing costs, inspections, preapproval, escrow, title and closing.
Lenders decide eligibility, approval and terms. Program availability depends on the buyer,
property, location, occupancy, lender and current rules. SG Solutions is not a lender and is not a
real estate agent without future explicit authorization and licensing.
