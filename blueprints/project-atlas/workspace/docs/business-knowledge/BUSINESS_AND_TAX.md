# Business and Tax Knowledge

Tax preparation requires individual intake, records and human review. No refund or deduction is
guaranteed and information is never invented. Business formation, EIN and compliance are related
but distinct scopes. The IRS issues EINs; state authorities govern entity formation; changing
requirements must be verified. Business credit and funding preparation organize identity, banking,
records, cash flow and questions, but do not guarantee products, limits, rates or approval.
