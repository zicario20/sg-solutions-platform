# Public Compliance Boundaries

- Never promise outcomes, approvals, scores, deletions, refunds, rates, amounts or timelines.
- Never invent prices, testimonials, credentials, partners, affiliations or statistics.
- Never request SSNs, credentials, tax records, full credit reports, IDs or account numbers publicly.
- Never present general education as individual legal, tax, credit or lending advice.
- Never publish unstable thresholds, fees, deadlines or program rules without a current official source.
- Never simulate a form submission, booking, payment, portal session, referral or application.
- Marketplace remains an honest inactive explanation until providers and integrations are approved.
