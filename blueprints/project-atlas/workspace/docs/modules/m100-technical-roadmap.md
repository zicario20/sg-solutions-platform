# M100 - Technical Roadmap

## Status

Technical controlled foundation implemented. Roadmap aggregation, automatic prioritization,
provider activation, deployment orchestration and change execution remain disabled. Product Owner
acceptance is pending.

## Canonical responsibility

M100 provides a versioned, evidence-led technical planning domain. It is not a task engine, build
system, provider controller, deployment engine or source of business authority.

## Implemented contracts

- Roadmaps and draft versions with no automatic publication or immutable-approval claim.
- Initiatives across foundation, enablement, scale, resilience and future horizons.
- Owner-module, dependency and evidence references without rewriting their canonical truth.
- Definition-of-done milestones that remain incomplete until evidence and approval exist.
- Explicit risks, Product Owner decision gates and review-required records.

## Safety rules

Technical completion is not commercial readiness, production approval or permission to activate a
provider. M099 owns release/deployment behavior; M101 owns commercial sequencing. Evidence is
reference-only and contracts reject credentials and private reasoning. The package cannot create
work, query telemetry, change priority, activate a provider or deploy software.

## Activation gate

Any operational roadmap capability requires Product Owner-approved governance, trusted
owner-module evidence, authorization and audit controls, and a separately approved rollout.
