# M064 - Source Management

## Status

Controlled, provider-disabled source registry and snapshot foundation implemented. Product Owner
acceptance and source acquisition/refresh activation remain pending.

## Boundary

M064 owns source identity, source records, versions, immutable snapshots, authority/trust state,
applicability references, freshness, and citation-support lineage. It does not curate knowledge,
rank retrieval results, parse document bytes, make legal precedence decisions, or run generic
crawlers.

## Fail-closed controls

Sources begin as drafts and snapshots begin captured-unverified and unpromoted. External content is
untrusted; latest retrieval is not effective truth; stale and unknown evidence is never marked
usable for a material decision. Fetches, connectors, parsing dispatch, promotion, jobs, and AI are
disabled.

## Enablement

Requires approved source governance, connector/SSRF security review, M065 parsing boundary,
immutable audit, M072/M068 controls, sandbox evidence, and separate Product Owner authorization.
