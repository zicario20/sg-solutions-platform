# M019 Organization Management - Provider-disabled technical baseline

- Owner: Codex Architecture Agent
- Final approver: Product Owner
- Status: Provider-disabled technical implementation complete; Product Owner acceptance pending
- Date: 2026-08-24
- Surface: internal Admin workspace and bounded domain/application contracts

M019 owns the future canonical Organization and person-organization relationship boundary. It is not
a Business Profile, CRM relationship, ClientRelationship, ServiceOrder, CaseFile, Filing Record,
Compliance Obligation, payment, document or provider integration. Those owners may later provide
minimized, purpose-authorized projections only.

The provider-disabled baseline adds typed organization/relationship/compliance/operations projection
contracts, purpose- and relationship-scoped authorization, version- and reauthentication-fenced state
policy, proposal controls, prohibited-field rejection, a fail-closed Admin route/API and bilingual
responsive UI. It creates no schema, migration, organization, relationship, ownership, registered
agent, filing, compliance obligation, document, payment, client access, EIN request or provider call.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- An ownership or officer label is not an access grant.
- A proposed organization cannot submit a filing, create an EIN or activate client access.
- Formation, EIN, filing, compliance and funding outcomes are not implied by organization state.
- Organization duplicates and merges require a future reviewed canonical-resolution workflow; no
  automatic merge, name-only match or AI-authorized merge exists here.
- Sensitive identifiers and contact, identity, tax, credit, document, financial and credential data
  are excluded from ordinary projections, URLs, telemetry and UI.
