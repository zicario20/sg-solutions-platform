# M101 - Business Roadmap

## Status

Technical controlled foundation implemented. Market research connections, KPI aggregation, CRM
access, campaign activation, pricing changes, partner activation, payment actions and commercial
execution remain disabled. Product Owner acceptance is pending.

## Canonical responsibility

M101 is a versioned business-planning and evidence-governance domain. It does not replace the
catalog, CRM, payments, compliance, fulfillment, technical roadmap or Product Owner authority.

## Implemented contracts

- Roadmaps and Waves 0-9 with explicit readiness/KPI references and no passed gate by default.
- Segment/customer-problem hypotheses marked unvalidated until supported by evidence.
- Offer readiness requiring catalog, operational, compliance and technical references, without
  enabling a public sale.
- KPI definitions that separate a plan to measure from data or an outcome claim.
- Capacity constraints, experiments/stop conditions, decision gates and continuity plans.

## Safety rules

Growth cannot override capacity, quality, compliance, consent, security, pricing or operational
readiness. M101 cannot create services, change prices, start campaigns, contact partners, collect
payments, activate products, make recommendations or claim revenue/customer outcomes. The module
accepts safe references only; it stores no credentials, personal data or private reasoning.

## Activation gate

Business experimentation or automation requires Product Owner approval, approved metrics and data
sources, M042/M046/M092 commercial and analytical boundaries, M076 compliance, M077 audit,
M078/M081/M082/M085 controls, and a bounded rollout with stop conditions.
