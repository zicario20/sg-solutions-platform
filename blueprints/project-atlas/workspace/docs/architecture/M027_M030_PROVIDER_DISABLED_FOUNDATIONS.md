# M027-M030 Provider-Disabled Foundations

M027 governs policy, privacy/retention disposition and risk assessment without making legal conclusions or deleting data. M028 defines privacy-preserving aggregate metrics only. M029 protects tradeline referrals, disclosures and provider tenant isolation without provider activation. M030 supplies a filing-readiness gate that can never transmit a return.

All external providers, secrets, operational records, migrations, production routes, client/tax/credit data, submissions, financial outcomes and deployment remain disabled. Product Owner acceptance remains pending.
