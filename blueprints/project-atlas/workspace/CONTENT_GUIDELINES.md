# Content Guidelines

- Owner: Content Approver role, initially the Product Owner
- Status: Active baseline
- Update rule: revise when services, legal review or bilingual terminology changes

Use plain, respectful English and Spanish. Explain eligibility and uncertainty. Never promise results, imply universal availability, fabricate testimonials or give individualized legal, tax, lending or credit guarantees. Every educational page identifies the next useful action and separates education from professional advice.
