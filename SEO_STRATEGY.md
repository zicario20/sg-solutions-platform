# SEO Strategy

- Owner: Growth Owner role, initially the Product Owner
- Status: Baseline
- Update rule: update after keyword research, Search Console evidence or material route changes

Build localized, intent-specific service and education pages with unique titles, descriptions, canonicals, hreflang, structured data and internal links. Financial Academy and Home Buying Center are durable topic clusters. Measure qualified evaluation requests, not vanity traffic; never send sensitive lead or portal content to analytics.
