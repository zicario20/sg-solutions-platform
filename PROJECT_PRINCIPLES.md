# Project Principles

- Owner: Product Owner
- Status: Active
- Update rule: changes require a dated rationale in `DECISIONS.md`

- Build one professional SG Solutions Platform, not disconnected applications.
- Never guarantee credit, lending, tax, mortgage, home-buying or funding outcomes.
- Prefer clarity over complexity and compatible extensions over rewrites.
- Use a modular monolith by default; extract a service only with documented boundary evidence.
- Reuse shared business primitives; verticals extend rather than duplicate them.
- Keep integrations replaceable behind provider adapters.
- Important flows work in English and Spanish.
- Clients always see current stage, missing items and next action.
- Education, security and privacy take priority over aggressive conversion.
- Access is explicit, least-privilege and revocable.
- Payment confirmation and AI output never replace required human authorization.
- Public prices are opt-in per service, sourced and disclosed; unpublished is the safe default.
- Cloud-first precedes homelab or hybrid complexity.
- `Registered` documents intent only; approved PRD and gates precede code.
